# VHeads <img src="assets/emoji/vheads-wave.png" alt="Mini Zoiila waving" width="29" height="29" />

## Small details, big personality <img src="assets/emoji/vheads-thinking.png" alt="Mini Zoiila thinking" width="25" height="25" />

**A custom head is a small block with a lot of visual character.**

Minecraft heads can work as accents in builds and themed spaces. A distinctive face, carefully selected texture, or subtle decorative arrangement can help an environment feel personal.

The examples below are **design ideas**, not claims about a built-in VHeads catalog, interface, commands, or supported integrations.

## Curate with intention <img src="assets/emoji/vheads-teacher.png" alt="Mini Zoiila sharing a design tip" width="25" height="25" />

<p align="center">
  <img src="assets/vheads-04-head-tips.svg" width="100%" alt="VHeads visual-design tips: purpose, theme, asset rights, and in-game review" />
</p>

A strong set of decorative heads starts with a purpose, not with collecting the largest possible number of textures.

- **Choose a purpose.** Know where and why a custom head belongs in your build.
- **Keep a visual theme.** Pick textures that fit the surrounding blocks, lighting, and scale.
- **Respect artwork rights.** Check permissions for textures or art you choose to reuse.
- **Review at Minecraft scale.** Test whether the detail is readable from a normal player viewpoint.

**Mini Zoiila's tip:** Start with a few strong details and expand only when they genuinely improve the space.
